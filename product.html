Product & Services (MVP)

A complete battery management system device delivering high-precision state estimation with reduced onboard sensors.

Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

SlimBatt will provide a complete battery management system device with both hardware and software. The MVP integrates our microcontroller and embedded proprietary estimation algorithms:

analytics
State Metric 01

State of Charge

(SoC)

Accurate real-time charge estimation across all cells derived from the average model, eliminating individual sensor dependency.

health_and_safety
State Metric 02

State of Health

(SoH)

Monitors cell aging, internal degradation, and health status over the operational lifecycle of the battery pack.

bolt
State Metric 03

State of Power

(SoP)

Calculates peak charging and discharging power capabilities in real time for safe, high-performance vehicle operation.

Core Product Advantages

check_circle Reduced Sensor Count: Fewer voltage sensors reduce bill-of-materials and physical failure points across cell strings.
check_circle Software Simplicity: Lean algorithmic design lowers MCU compute requirements and simplifies software maintenance.
check_circle Uncompromised Accuracy: Retains the same SoC estimation accuracy while operating with significantly fewer sensors.
check_circle Cost Effectiveness: Simultaneous hardware and software reduction makes SlimBatt significantly more cost-effective.
