Market Analysis

The proposed technology will directly benefit the battery and automotive industries by improving the reliability of EV batteries.

Segment 01 Standalone Product Sales

Sold directly as a standalone product to battery companies, OEMs, and suppliers who do not design BMS in-house.

Segment 02 Core Tech Licensing

Licensed to potential customers in different battery sectors where customers design in-house BMS themselves.

Segment 03 Tier 2 Suppliers

Tier 2 suppliers who provide consulting services on battery and BMS could also be potential buyers.

rocket_launch

Go-to-Market Plan

A phased customer adoption model designed to validate commercial viability before scaling to high-volume production.

Initial Target Phase Phase 1: Component Suppliers

Focus initially on suppliers and EV battery companies who do not design their in-house BMS to develop a viable business model.

Scaling Phase Phase 2: OEM Scale

Target expansion towards major automotive OEMs for larger market share and vehicle platform integration.

Short-Term Plan

A clearly defined engineering and industrial demonstration timeline:

Current State ~15V Lab Prototype

Working lab-scale prototype with software enhancements scheduled over the next few months.

1–2 Years ~150V Partner Testing

Collaborate with industrial partners to prototype and demonstrate in an industrial setting.

2–3 Years Industrial Prototype

Deliver a working industrial prototype ready for full commercial deployment.

Industry Impact & Value Proposition

By reducing the required number of onboard sensors without compromising battery management system functionality, SlimBatt directly tackles the reliability and cost challenges of electric vehicle batteries. Reducing both hardware complexity and algorithmic computational overhead creates a competitive advantage for automakers and battery suppliers alike.
