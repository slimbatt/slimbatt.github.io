Next-Generation BMS Innovation

Sensor-Lean
BMS Technology

Electric vehicle battery management systems heavily rely on onboard voltage sensors, creating reliability concerns when sensors fail. SlimBatt reduces dependency on onboard sensors by combining hardware sensor reduction with simplified state estimation algorithms—retaining accuracy while cutting complexity.
