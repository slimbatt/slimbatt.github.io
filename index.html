BMS Technology

Our technology reduces dependency on onboard voltage sensors. Hardware and wiring represent 5-10% of battery cost. We replace these with advanced algorithms.

The Problem & Our Solution

Addressing hardware complexity with intelligent, algorithm-driven estimation.

warning

The Problem

  • close Batteries need individual sensors for each cell. This adds hardware and software complexity.
  • close More sensors mean more cost, space requirements, and more failure points.
memory

Our Solution

  • check Instead of measuring every cell, we measure strings of cells.
  • check We use an "average model" to estimate individual cell conditions via advanced algorithms.

Commercialization

Market Analysis

Target customers include EV manufacturers (OEMs), battery pack manufacturers, and Tier 1 BMS suppliers. The value proposition is cost reduction and simplification of the battery pack architecture.

Go-to Market Plan

B2B licensing model or direct component sales (selling the programmed MCU). Initial focus on pilot projects with early adopters to validate the technology in real-world scenarios.

Short-term Plan

Scale up from 15V lab scale to 150V. We are planning to build hardware for industrial validation.
