SlimBatt LLC

SlimBatt is an early-stage startup developing a novel sensor-lean technology for real-time state estimation in multi-cell lithium-ion battery packs. The technology, developed under NSF Grant No. ECCS-2237317 and the subject of a pending non-provisional patent application (claiming priority to U.S. Provisional Application No. 63/795,912), enables computational-efficient state estimation on resource-constrained embedded processors using a single voltage sensor.
